# Visitor journey

The scoping deck was used as a strategy reference, not as a set of approved factual claims. The hierarchy is community first, with the spiritual tradition explained deeper in the site. The full requested website name is preserved.

1. Arrive: three people-led hero slides say who the community welcomes. The main action is always Plan your first visit.
2. Explore: three visible routes cover gatherings and connection, wellbeing and reflection, and learning or contributing. They lead to real public pages.
3. Connect: a first-visit guide, a three-step visit section and gathering enquiries reduce uncertainty. The primary link preselects First visit on the contact form. Dates and venue are confirmed before travelling.
4. Support: volunteering and donations appear later in the homepage, after visitors understand the community. Existing supporters can still use the footer links directly.

No conversion counts, testimonials, guaranteed services, confirmed event dates or response-time promises were invented. The current forms prepare email drafts; real delivery, registrations and payment completion require the backend described separately. Production measurement should track first-visit CTA clicks, enquiry completion, event interest, confirmed attendance and verified donations without collecting unnecessary personal data.
